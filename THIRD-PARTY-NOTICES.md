# Third-party notices

## IEEE Registration Authority public listings

The CSV files in `internal/discovery/vendors` contain public MAC address
assignment registry data published by the IEEE Registration Authority:

- MA-L: <https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui/oui.csv>
- MA-M: <https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui28/mam.csv>
- MA-S: <https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui36/oui36.csv>

Source: <https://standards.ieee.org/products-programs/regauth/>

These are third-party registry data, not original project code, and are not
relicensed under this project's MIT license. IEEE names and marks belong to
their respective owners. Inclusion does not imply endorsement. The embedded
listings are snapshots and can become outdated.
